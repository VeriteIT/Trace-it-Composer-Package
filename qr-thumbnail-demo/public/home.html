What is happening here

  1. The CMS publishes an article and assigns it an ID.
  2. It POSTs just that ID to our service — no article data, no S3 access.
  3. We mint a Trace-It code and store it against their ID.
  4. On the page, our script reads data-article-id from the thumbnail.
  5. It requests /v1/qr/<id>.png from us and places it in the image frame.

Which images get a code

Only images matching data-selector — here img.story-thumb carrying data-article-id. Logos, ads and inline body images are never touched.

To exclude one thumbnail that would otherwise match, put data-traceit="off" on it. The third story below has exactly that, so it keeps its plain photo.

No CORS anywhere

Nothing in the browser ever reads the photo's pixels, so the S3 bucket needs no Access-Control-Allow-Origin header in either mode. The compositing happens on our server, where same-origin policy does not apply.
